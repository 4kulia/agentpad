# AgentPad Privacy Policy


**Effective date:** October 9, 2026

AgentPad is an iPhone app for your organization's work chat. Its developer is Andrey Kulikov, an individual.

**Data and purpose.** AgentPad processes data linked to your account to provide chat, sign-in, synchronization and security:

- Your email address, account ID, handle, display name and organization memberships.
- Messages, attachments and related chat data, such as mentions, reactions and read status. The first iPhone release is planned to support viewing and downloading attachments already shared in the organization.
- Device name, session identifiers, sign-in IP address when available, and security records used to manage access and prevent abuse.

**Storage and responsibility.** Account and chat data are stored on the AgentPad server your organization uses. The app keeps an on-device cache of chat data and connection details; its sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain. Messages and files are available to members with access to the relevant channels.

If your organization runs its own server, it is the data controller for the information stored there. Contact it about access, retention and deletion of its work records.

**Hosted server operator:** Andrey Kulikov (individual), andrew@rabbitshat.ai. The server is hosted by HOSTKEY in the Netherlands.

**Other services.** A server configured to use SendGrid sends it your email address and email contents to deliver sign-in codes and invitations. Email open and click tracking are disabled. AgentPad has no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party analytics.

**Push notifications (planned).** When available and enabled, the server will store an account-linked Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token, notification preferences and app registration details to deliver notifications through Apple. Notification content, including message text by default, will pass through APNs. You will be able to turn off message text in notification settings.

**Account deletion (planned).** Settings → Delete Account will be available even without an organization. Across your organizations on that server, your member record, messages, files and name as their author will remain as organizational work records. Your profile will be marked deactivated and you will be removed from teams. Your email will be removed from the account, all sessions ended and deleted, and push tokens revoked and deleted. The email address can then be invited again.

To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact your organization's administrator or **andrew@rabbitshat.ai**, giving your server address and account email. See [Support](support.md).
