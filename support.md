# AgentPad Support


AgentPad for iPhone is developed by Andrey Kulikov, an individual.

**Contact:** andrew@rabbitshat.ai

For app problems, include your app version, iOS version, server address, what you were doing and any error message. Do not include sign-in codes or session tokens. Remove private chat content from screenshots.

For an invitation, a missing sign-in email or access to your organization's channels, contact your organization's administrator. Check that you are using its server address and the email address that was invited.

**Request account deletion.** Contact your organization's administrator or andrew@rabbitshat.ai with your server address and account email, and state that you want to delete your account. In-app deletion is planned at Settings → Delete Account, including for accounts with no organizations. A last owner of an organization with other members must transfer ownership first using AgentPad for Mac; ask your administrator for help if needed.

Under the planned deletion process, your messages, files, author name and member record remain with your organizations as work records. Your profile is marked deactivated and you are removed from teams. Your email is removed from the account, all sessions are ended and deleted, and push tokens are revoked and deleted.

For concerns about a message or file, contact your organization's administrator. An organization running its own server controls the data stored there. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) for data handling and hosted server operator details.
